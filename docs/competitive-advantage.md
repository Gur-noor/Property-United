# What No One Else Is Doing

## The Market Gap

The tokenized real estate market is growing rapidly. Numerous platforms are actively raising capital to acquire properties using crypto infrastructure. Yet every one of them — regardless of how sophisticated their technology — follows the same model when it comes to acquiring properties:

They raise investor capital, convert it to cash or stablecoins, buy the property from the seller in fiat currency, and the seller walks away permanently. The platform keeps all future income and all future appreciation. The seller's economic relationship with their property ends the moment the deal closes.

**No platform currently offers sellers a genuine alternative to walking away forever.**

United Properties TRU™ is built around a different model entirely.

---

## 1. Sellers Become Partners — Not Just Sellers

When any buyer acquires a property — whether an individual, a fund, a REIT, or a tokenized real estate platform — the seller faces the same outcome: take the cash and lose every future dollar that property will ever generate.

United Properties TRU™ offers sellers a choice that no other buyer in the market currently provides:

**Option A — Cash:** Standard transaction. Seller receives cash and exits. All future income and appreciation belongs to the buyer from that point forward.

**Option B — Cash + Tokens (any mix):** Instead of selling for all-cash and walking away from every future dollar, an owner can take part or all of their proceeds in tokens — choosing **any combination of cash and tokens** (all cash, all tokens, or a split such as 70/30 or 50/50). To the extent they take tokens, they become a partner in a growing, professionally managed portfolio — continuing to earn income and participate in appreciation across many properties, in many locations, rather than the single property they sold.

When a seller accepts tokens:

- They receive a fractional interest in the **entire platform portfolio** — not just the property they sold
- They continue earning **rental income** from all platform properties
- They continue **participating in appreciation** across all platform properties in multiple locations
- They gain access to **compliant secondary market liquidity** — something they never had as a sole property owner
- They diversify from a single concentrated asset into a managed, growing portfolio

This creates a new class of participant: the **seller-partner** — a former property owner who becomes an ongoing co-investor in the platform rather than a one-time cash recipient.

:::info No one else does this
Every other real estate buyer — including every tokenized real estate platform on the market — acquires properties in exchange for cash. The seller always walks away. United Properties TRU™ is building the only model that lets sellers stay in the game.
:::

| Keep the property | None | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Other tokenized platforms | Limited, per-property | On that property only | On that property only |
| **United Properties TRU™** | **Growing (Phase 4 secondary market)** | **✅ Entire portfolio** | **✅ Entire portfolio** |

Through a compliant secondary market planned for Phase 4, token holders will be able to transfer a portion of their position to another verified investor — accessing liquidity — while their remaining tokens continue to earn income and appreciate across the full portfolio.

This is the combination no other platform currently offers: the ability to get liquidity from your position without being forced to permanently exit your income and appreciation stream.

## 3. Investors Own a Diversified Portfolio — Not a Single Property

Other platforms ask investors to select specific individual properties. This forces concentration risk, requires individual due diligence the typical investor is not equipped to perform well, and eliminates the proven benefits of diversification.

United Properties TRU™ investors do not pick properties. **They own a fractionalized interest in a diversified pool of income-producing and appreciating residential rental properties** across different geographic locations and market conditions.

This means:

- Returns are based on the **combined performance of multiple properties across multiple markets** — not one asset in one location
- **Geographic diversification** reduces exposure to any single local market downturn
- **Professional selection and management** across the full portfolio — not investor guesswork on individual deals
- **Reduced concentration risk** — one property's vacancy or underperformance does not define the investor's return

This is how institutional allocators access real estate. United Properties TRU™ makes that model available at qualifying retail minimum sizes.

## 4. Comparative Advantages — vs. Crypto & Stablecoins

> **Better than Bitcoin — because it's asset-backed.**
> **Better than Tether — because its assets produce income.**

United Properties TRU™ combines the accessibility of a digital asset with the fundamentals of real estate. For holders of Bitcoin, Tether, and other speculative cryptocurrencies, it offers a way to move from non-productive digital assets into an interest backed by real, income-producing property.

### Bitcoin: No Income, No Hard Asset Backing

Bitcoin is not backed by any physical or hard asset — no real estate, no gold, no government. It produces zero income for holders. Its valuation is based entirely on speculation, network effects, and scarcity — not on any underlying cash flow that can be modeled or measured. A Bitcoin holder's return depends entirely on finding a future buyer willing to pay more.

### Tether: Earns Income — But Keeps It All

Tether USDT generates significant returns from the US Treasury Bills that back most of its reserves. But Tether keeps all of that yield. USDT holders earn nothing on their holdings — they are effectively lending their capital to Tether and receiving only a dollar peg in return, while Tether earns the interest income on their deposits.

| Appreciation | Speculative only | ❌ Pegged to USD | ✅ Real property appreciation |
| Supply | Approaching maximum | Unlimited, centralized | ✅ Backed by real property (issued per asset) |
| Regulatory clarity | Uncertain | Counterparty + freeze risk | ✅ Reg D compliant |

For a Bitcoin or Tether holder who wants an asset that actually earns income, is backed by tangible property, and sits within a clear regulatory structure, United Properties TRU™ offers exactly that: a real-estate-backed interest that pays rental income and participates in appreciation.
