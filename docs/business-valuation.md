United Properties TRU™ is, at its core, a **real estate rollup**: as more properties are acquired into the diversified pool, both the underlying portfolio and the platform that manages it grow in value. This section outlines how the business is valued and the scale it is designed to reach.

## How the Business Is Valued

The platform's value is assessed using three complementary methodologies:

| Methodology | What it measures | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Net Asset Value (NAV)** | The aggregate value of the properties held in the pool, net of liabilities | Anchors token value to real, asset-backed equity |
| **Income Capitalization** | The portfolio's net operating income (NOI) divided by a market capitalization rate | Values the pool as an income-producing enterprise |
| **Platform Liquidity Multiple** | A multiple applied to platform fee revenue and AUM growth | Captures the value of the platform/GP layer as a scalable business |

NAV and income capitalization value the **pool** (what token holders own). The liquidity multiple values the **platform** (the GP / PlatformCo layer that owns the IP and earns fees).
