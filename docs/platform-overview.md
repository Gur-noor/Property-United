# Platform Overview

## How the Platform Works

United Properties TRU™ operates as a managed platform connecting verified accredited investors to fractional ownership interests in income-producing real estate. The platform handles the full lifecycle of each property — from initial onboarding through to investor distributions and eventual disposition.

The following describes the end-to-end operational flow:

---

## Step 1 — Property Sourcing and Structuring

A property is identified for inclusion on the platform. Once approved for listing, a new series is created within PropCo Master (the Delaware Series LLC managed by PlatformCo). The property's legal title is transferred into the new series.

At this stage:
- The series is legally isolated from all other series within PropCo Master
- PlatformCo earns an **origination / acquisition fee** of 1–3% of property value at close
- A new set of ERC-3643 property tokens is prepared for issuance to represent membership interests in the series

---

## Step 2 — Token Issuance and Listing

The series membership interests are represented as ERC-3643 tokens. The total token supply for a given property corresponds to the total equity value of the property, divided into purchasable units.

At this stage:
- PlatformCo earns a **tokenization / listing fee** of 0.5–2% at the time of token mint
- The property is made available to verified investors on the platform
- All token transfers are restricted to KYC-whitelisted wallets from the moment of issuance

---

## Step 3 — Investor Verification and Onboarding

Prospective investors complete identity verification (KYC) and business verification (KYB) through the platform's verification process. Accredited investor status is confirmed prior to any investment being accepted.

Upon successful verification:
- The investor's wallet is added to the on-chain whitelist for the relevant property series
- The investor is permitted to purchase tokens in the Regulation D 506(c) offering
- Investment records are maintained both on-chain and in off-chain records

---

## Step 4 — Capital Raise and Token Distribution

The property series conducts its Reg D 506(c) offering. Verified investors transfer capital and receive ERC-3643 property tokens in return. Token holdings represent the investor's proportional ownership interest in the series and, by extension, in the underlying property.

---

## Step 5 — Ongoing Asset Management and Distributions

PlatformCo manages each property series on an ongoing basis. Rental income generated by the property is collected and distributed to token holders pro-rata by on-chain token snapshot, on a monthly or quarterly schedule.

Distributions are made in USDC or fiat. PlatformCo earns an **asset management fee** of 1–2% of annual rent per series.

---

## Step 6 — Secondary Transfers

Token holders who wish to transfer their position may do so through whitelisted peer-to-peer transfers, subject to the on-chain compliance controls of the ERC-3643 standard. Both the sender and recipient must hold active KYC-whitelisted wallet status.

PlatformCo earns a **secondary transfer fee** of 0.25–1% per whitelisted trade.

A formal secondary market via a licensed venue or partner is planned for a later phase of the roadmap.

---

## Step 7 — Property Exit

When a property is sold, proceeds are distributed to token holders pro-rata. PlatformCo earns a **disposition fee** of 1–2% of the sale price. The series is subsequently wound down.

---

## Platform Revenue Summary

| Fee | Rate | When Earned |
|---|---|---|
| Origination / acquisition | 1–3% of property value | At each property close |
| Tokenization / listing | 0.5–2% | At token mint |
| Asset management | 1–2% of rent / yr | Ongoing, per series |
| Disposition | 1–2% of sale price | At property exit |
| Secondary transfer | 0.25–1% | Per whitelisted trade |

Platform revenue scales directly with Assets Under Management (AUM). An illustrative portfolio of 10 properties at an average value of $300,000 ($3M AUM) generates approximately $80,000 per year in platform revenue. At $30M AUM, the annualised run-rate approaches $800,000. Revenue growth is driven by AUM growth, not by headcount expansion.
