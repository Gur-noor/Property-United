# Platform Overview

## How the Platform Works

United Properties TRU™ operates as a managed platform connecting verified accredited investors to fractional ownership interests in income-producing real estate. The platform handles the full lifecycle of each property — from initial onboarding through to investor distributions and eventual disposition.

The following describes the end-to-end operational flow:

---

## Step 1 — Property Sourcing and Structuring

A property is identified for inclusion on the platform. Once approved for listing, the property is placed in its own isolated, bankruptcy-remote legal structure held for the Partnership. The property's legal title is transferred into that structure.

At this stage:
- The property is legally isolated from every other property in the portfolio
- The Partnership earns an **origination / acquisition fee** of 1–3% of property value at close
- ERC-3643 UPTRU property tokens are prepared for minting into the shared pool to represent a diversified interest in the whole portfolio

---

## Step 2 — Token Issuance and Listing

Portfolio interests are represented as ERC-3643 UPTRU tokens. As each property is added, tokens corresponding to that property's equity value are minted into the single shared pool.

At this stage:
- The Partnership earns a **tokenization / listing fee** of 0.5–2% at the time of token mint
- The expanded portfolio is made available to verified investors on the platform
- All token transfers are restricted to KYC-whitelisted wallets from the moment of issuance

---

## Step 3 — Investor Verification and Onboarding

Prospective investors complete identity verification (KYC) and business verification (KYB) through the platform's verification process. Accredited investor status is confirmed prior to any investment being accepted.

Upon successful verification:
- The investor is admitted to the Regulation D 506(c) offering and may subscribe
- In Phase 1, the investor's position is recorded off-chain with a pre-assigned UPTRU allocation; the investor's wallet is added to the on-chain whitelist in Phase 2+, when tokens are minted
- Investment records are maintained in off-chain records (and on-chain from Phase 2+)

---

## Step 4 — Capital Raise and Allocation

The Partnership conducts its Reg D 506(c) offering. Verified investors transfer capital and receive a position in the Partnership representing a proportional, diversified ownership interest in the whole portfolio — not any single underlying property. In Phase 1 this position is recorded off-chain as a pre-assigned UPTRU allocation; from Phase 2+, that allocation is minted as ERC-3643 UPTRU property tokens and delivered to the investor's whitelisted wallet.

---

## Step 5 — Ongoing Asset Management and Distributions

The Partnership (through its management layer) manages the portfolio on an ongoing basis. Rental income generated across all properties is collected and distributed to token holders pro-rata by on-chain token snapshot, on a monthly or quarterly schedule.

Distributions are made in USDC or fiat. The Partnership earns an **asset management fee** of 1–2% of annual rent.

---

## Step 6 — Secondary Transfers

Token holders who wish to transfer their position may do so through whitelisted peer-to-peer transfers, subject to the on-chain compliance controls of the ERC-3643 standard. Both the sender and recipient must hold active KYC-whitelisted wallet status.

The Partnership earns a **secondary transfer fee** of 0.25–1% per whitelisted trade.

A formal secondary market via a licensed venue or partner is planned for a later phase of the roadmap.

---

## Step 7 — Property Exit

When a property is sold, net proceeds flow into the portfolio and are distributed to token holders pro-rata. The Partnership earns a **disposition fee** of 1–2% of the sale price. The property's isolated structure is subsequently wound down.

---

## Platform Revenue Summary

| Fee | Rate | When Earned |
|---|---|---|
| Origination / acquisition | 1–3% of property value | At each property close |
| Tokenization / listing | 0.5–2% | At token mint |
| Asset management | 1–2% of rent / yr | Ongoing, across the portfolio |
| Disposition | 1–2% of sale price | At property exit |
| Secondary transfer | 0.25–1% | Per whitelisted trade |

Platform revenue scales directly with Assets Under Management (AUM). An illustrative portfolio of 10 properties at an average value of $300,000 ($3M AUM) generates approximately $80,000 per year in platform revenue. At $30M AUM, the annualised run-rate approaches $800,000. Revenue growth is driven by AUM growth, not by headcount expansion.
