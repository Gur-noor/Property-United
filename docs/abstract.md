# Abstract

> **Version 1.1 — Draft for Review** · June 2026

**United Properties TRU™** is a compliant real estate tokenization platform built around one thing no one else in the market offers: **liquidity for property owners without giving up future rental income or appreciation.**

Instead of selling to an institutional buyer for all-cash and walking away from every future dollar, an owner can **exchange their property for tokens** and become a partner in a growing, professionally managed portfolio — continuing to earn income and participate in appreciation across **many** properties, in **many** locations, rather than the single property they sold.

This makes United Properties TRU™ a **tokenized real estate rollup**: a platform that acquires income-producing residential rental properties (with tokens and/or cash), aggregates them into a diversified pool, and unlocks the trillions of dollars currently trapped and illiquid in the U.S. residential rental market.

## What Makes Us Different

:::tip[The core differentiator]
Everyone else buys a property and keeps **all** of its future income and appreciation. United Properties lets the seller keep participating — converting an illiquid single asset into a liquid, diversified, income-producing position. **Liquidity *and* upside, not one or the other.**
:::

## The Two Sides of the Platform

- **Sellers (property owners)** exchange their rental property for tokens. They move from active landlord to passive investor — deferring an all-cash taxable exit, shedding management and vacancy risk, and keeping exposure to income and appreciation across the whole portfolio.
- **Investors (token holders)** acquire a **fractionalized interest in a diversified pool** of income-producing and appreciating residential rental properties — across property types and geographies. They are **not** picking one building; they own a slice of the portfolio, so returns are diversified by design.

## The Token Model

The platform operates a layered token model:

- **Property (Asset) Tokens** — ERC-3643 permissioned tokens representing a fractional interest in the **diversified pool** of properties. Holders earn pro-rata rental income (in USDC or fiat) and gain exposure to the appreciation of the whole portfolio.
- **Utility Token ($TRU)** — A platform-level token (1,000,000,000 fixed supply) providing fee discounts, priority allocation, loyalty benefits, and governance. Issued in Phase 5 only.
- **General Partnership (GP) interests** — A pre-launch tier (PlatformCo equity) that owns the platform's intellectual property and trade secrets and earns all platform fees.

## Phase 1 Scope

Phase 1 (**currently in development**) is intentionally off-chain. It delivers:

1. A public landing page, plus **seller** and **investor** education portals
2. Investor and seller registration with full KYC/KYB and AML screening
3. Accredited investor verification (Reg D 506(c))
4. Two offering tracks — LP-interest subscriptions (PPM / Subscription / LP Agreement) and a future security-token / SAFT track
5. The **seller acquisition workflow** and an authorized **agent/Realtor** network
6. Admin dashboard and off-chain records

No tokens are minted in Phase 1. The platform transitions to on-chain operations in Phase 2 with the first tokenized property pool.

## In One Paragraph

United Properties TRU™ is a tokenized real estate rollup that gives the ~20 million mostly small "mom-and-pop" owners of America's ~50 million residential rental units a way to **cash out without cashing out** — exchanging an illiquid property for a liquid, diversified token position that keeps paying income and appreciating. By combining compliant legal structures (Delaware C-Corp + Series LLC), regulated securities offerings (Reg D 506(c)), and permissioned blockchain tokens (ERC-3643), the platform aggregates individual rentals into a diversified, professionally managed pool — delivering liquidity to sellers, diversification to investors, and a new listing tool to real estate agents, all without anyone giving up future income and appreciation.
