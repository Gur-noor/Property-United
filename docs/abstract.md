# Abstract

> **Version 1.0 — Draft for Review** · June 2026

**United Properties TRU™** is a compliant, fractional tokenized real estate ownership platform designed to give verified investors access to institutional-quality real estate — at any ticket size — through regulated securities and blockchain technology.

The platform operates under a **two-token model**:

- **Property Tokens** — ERC-3643 permissioned tokens representing fractional ownership of a specific, legally-structured property entity. Investors earn pro-rata rental income and gain exposure to property value appreciation.
- **Utility Token ($TRU)** — A future platform-level token providing fee discounts, priority allocation rights, loyalty benefits, and governance participation. Issued in Phase 5 only.

## Phase 1 Scope

Phase 1 (**currently in development**) is intentionally off-chain. It delivers:

1. A public landing page and investor education portal
2. Investor registration with full KYC/KYB and AML screening
3. Accredited investor verification (Reg D 506(c))
4. SAFT agreement flow with token warrant rights
5. Admin dashboard and off-chain investor records

No tokens are minted in Phase 1. The platform transitions to on-chain operations in Phase 2 with the first tokenized property raise.

## In One Paragraph

United Properties TRU™ removes the barriers that have locked most people out of real estate investing — high minimums, illiquidity, opacity, and geographic friction — by combining compliant legal structures (Delaware C-Corp + Series LLC), regulated securities offerings (Reg D 506(c)), and permissioned blockchain tokens (ERC-3643). Verified investors can own a fraction of a property, earn rental yield on-chain, and — eventually — trade their position on a compliant secondary market.
