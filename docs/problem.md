# The Problem

Real estate consistently outperforms other asset classes over long time horizons — yet it remains stubbornly inaccessible and inefficient for the vast majority of potential investors.

## 1. High Capital Requirements

Buying investment property outright requires:
- A 20–30% down payment (often $60k–$300k+)
- Mortgage qualification with income/credit thresholds
- Significant transaction costs: agent commissions, legal fees, title insurance, taxes

This makes real estate effectively unavailable to anyone who hasn't already accumulated substantial wealth.

## 2. Limited Deal Access

The best real estate opportunities — commercial properties, multi-family buildings, development deals — are shared through private networks. Retail investors have no visibility, let alone access.

## 3. Manual, Opaque Processes

The current model relies on a chain of intermediaries: brokers, escrow agents, title companies, property managers, accountants. Each adds cost, delay, and an opportunity for error or misalignment. There is no single source of truth for ownership or income.

## 4. Illiquidity

Selling real estate takes months. Capital is locked. Investors cannot rebalance or exit when their needs change. This illiquidity commands a discount to intrinsic value.

## 5. Administrative Complexity

Managing a rental property is a business: leases, maintenance, taxes, insurance, compliance, tenant management. Most investors simply don't have the time or expertise.

## 6. Geographic Constraints

Diversifying across markets requires local relationships, local knowledge, and often a local legal presence. Investing internationally is even harder.

## Summary

| Pain Point | Traditional Real Estate | Investor Need |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum investment | Full property value | Fractional, low minimum |
| Deal access | Private networks only | Open, transparent marketplace |
| Transparency | Opaque, intermediated | Verifiable on-chain records |
| Liquidity | Months to sell | Trade or transfer anytime |
| Management | Full burden on owner | Passive, managed for you |
| Geography | Local / relationships | Global, permissionless\* |

\* *Subject to applicable regulation — see the [legal disclaimer](./legal-disclaimer.md).*
