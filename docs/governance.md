# Governance

:::note[Phase 5]
Formal on-chain governance activates in **Phase 5** when $TRU is launched. In earlier phases, the platform is governed by the founding team under a core multisig with transparency commitments.
:::

## Pre-DAO Governance (Phases 0–4)

During the platform's early phases, operational decisions are made by:

- **Core team** — day-to-day product and business decisions
- **Core multisig** — smart contract upgrades and treasury actions (Phase 2+)
- **Investor advisory input** — SAFT investors have first-allocation rights and informal advisory influence

All significant decisions affecting investors (fee changes, new property structures, compliance policy changes) are communicated with advance notice.

## DAO Governance (Phase 5+)

When $TRU launches, governance transitions to a token-holder DAO. $TRU holders can propose and vote on:

- Onboarding and delisting of property originators
- Platform fee parameters
- Treasury allocation and grants
- Smart contract upgrades
- Strategic partnerships and integrations
- $TRU token utility changes

## Governance Process

1. **Temperature check** — informal forum discussion to gauge community interest.
2. **Formal proposal** — submitted on-chain with a required $TRU proposal threshold.
3. **Voting period** — token-weighted voting over a fixed window.
4. **Timelock** — passed proposals enter a timelock before execution, giving the community time to respond.
5. **Execution** — automated on-chain execution or multisig execution for off-chain actions.

## Progressive Decentralization Commitment

United Properties TRU™ is committed to handing control to the community over time. The roadmap explicitly targets full DAO governance by Phase 5. Control is transferred in stages as the system matures and the community grows — not all at once, and not before the system is ready.
