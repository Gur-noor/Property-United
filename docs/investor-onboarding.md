# Investor Onboarding Journey

The United Properties TRU™ onboarding process is designed to be thorough, compliant, and as frictionless as possible for verified investors. Every step is required by applicable law or necessary for platform integrity.

### Step 1 — Landing Page Visit

The investor discovers the platform via the public landing page. They review the platform overview, investment thesis, and educational content about tokenized real estate and the TRU™ model.

**Investor action:** Express interest / click "Get Started"

---

### Step 2 — Investor Education

Before registering, the investor completes a guided education module covering:

- How property tokenization works
- The two-token model (Property Tokens vs $TRU)
- Risks and risk disclosures
- Regulatory framework (Reg D 506(c))
- What "accredited investor" means and requires

**Investor action:** Complete education flow, acknowledge understanding

---

### Step 3 — Registration

The investor creates an account:

- Full legal name
- Email address and password
- Country of residence
- Investor type (individual or entity)

**Platform action:** Account created, verification email sent

---

### Step 4 — KYC / KYB

Identity verification is completed via our third-party KYC/KYB provider (Sumsub or Persona):

**Individual investors (KYC):**
- Government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license)
- Selfie / liveness check
- Address verification

**Entity investors (KYB):**
- Entity formation documents
- Ownership structure / beneficial owner declarations
- Authorized signatory verification

**Platform action:** AML screening against global watchlists; sanctions check; PEP screening

---

### Step 5 — Accreditation Verification

The investor provides documentation to verify accredited investor status under Reg D:

**Income path:** Two years of income documentation (tax returns, W-2s, pay stubs) showing $200k+ individual / $300k+ joint income

**Net worth path:** Documentation showing $1M+ net worth excluding primary residence (brokerage statements, bank statements, CPA letter)

**Professional certification path:** Active Series 7, 65, or 82 license; registered investment adviser; knowledgeable employee of a qualifying fund

**Platform action:** Admin review of documents; approval or request for additional materials

---

### Step 6 — Risk Disclosure Acknowledgment

The investor reviews and acknowledges the platform's risk disclosure documents:

- General investment risk
- Real estate specific risk
- Regulatory and liquidity risk
- Technology and smart contract risk
- SAFT and utility token risk

**Investor action:** Explicit acknowledgment (checkbox, e-signature, or both)

---

### Step 7 — Commitment Form

The investor indicates:

- Intended investment amount
- Preferred investment vehicle (specific property or general interest)
- Source of funds declaration
- Investment objectives

**Platform action:** Commitment logged; investor flagged for SAFT flow

---

### Step 8 — SAFT Agreement

The investor executes a **Simple Agreement for Future Tokens (SAFT)** with a token warrant side letter:

- Reviews SAFT terms (pro-rata $TRU rights at TGE)
- Reviews SAFE investment terms (if applicable)
- E-signature via DocuSign or equivalent
- Receives countersigned copy

**Platform action:** SAFT recorded in investor database, token warrant allocation reserved

---

### Step 9 — Wallet Submission (Optional in Phase 1)

Investors optionally submit a wallet address for future token delivery. This is not required in Phase 1 (no tokens are issued yet) but is recommended for a smooth Phase 2 onboarding.

**Platform action:** Wallet address recorded; whitelisting queued for Phase 2

---

### Step 10 — Admin Review Period

The admin team performs a final review of the investor's full application:

- KYC/KYB pass confirmed
- Accreditation status verified
- SAFT fully executed
- No compliance flags

Typical review time: 2–5 business days

---

### Step 11 — Approved

The investor is approved and gains access to the investor portal:

- View available property opportunities
- Track application/investment status
- Receive distribution notifications
- Participate in future property offerings when live

**Welcome email sent with portal access details.**

---

## Admin Operations

Throughout the onboarding journey, the admin team has access to a dashboard with:

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Investor list | All investors, status filter, search |
| Status workflows | Pending → Under Review → Approved / Rejected |
| Document tracking | View uploaded documents, request missing items |
| Internal notes | Per-investor notes for admin team |
| Audit trail | Timestamped log of every admin action |
| AML flags | Escalation queue for any AML screening hits |
| Bulk export | Investor data export for regulatory reporting |
