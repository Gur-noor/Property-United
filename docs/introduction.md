# Introduction

Real estate is the world's largest asset class — estimated at over **$300 trillion** globally. Yet for most people it remains one of the most inaccessible: minimum investments are enormous, deals are slow to find and close, liquidity is near-zero, and the administrative burden is relentless.

The traditional model is broken for three groups in particular:

- **Retail and emerging investors** who want exposure to real estate income but can't afford a down payment or qualify for a mortgage.
- **High-net-worth individuals** who want diversified real estate positions across multiple properties and geographies without the complexity of direct ownership.
- **Property owners and developers** who want to unlock liquidity from assets without a full sale.

## What United Properties TRU™ Does

United Properties TRU™ is a **compliant, fractionalized real estate ownership platform**. We:

1. **Source and structure** income-producing properties inside bankruptcy-remote legal entities (Delaware Series LLC series).
2. **Tokenize** fractional ownership using ERC-3643 permissioned tokens, each representing a proportional stake in a specific property.
3. **Raise capital** via Regulation D 506(c) offerings — verified, accredited US investors only.
4. **Distribute yield** — rental income flows to token holders in USDC, automatically, on a monthly or quarterly schedule.
5. **Enable secondary liquidity** — whitelisted token transfers and (in later phases) a licensed secondary marketplace.
